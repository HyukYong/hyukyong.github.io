SPECTROMASTER PRIVACY POLICY
Version 2026-08-27

Publisher: Qemica Lab, an independent software developer based in the
Republic of Korea ("we").
Contact: feedback.qemica@gmail.com  ·  We are a one-person operation and
have not appointed a separate data protection officer. Email is our
contact channel; we publish no postal address.

This policy covers the Spectromaster application for iPhone, iPad and
Mac. It does NOT cover the Qemica desktop workbench (/legal/privacy) or
Chembook (/legal/chembook-privacy), which are separate products with
their own policies.


1. SUMMARY

   Spectromaster does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
   data. We cannot see your spectra, your samples, or anything you do
   in the app.

   Reading and interpreting a spectrum happens entirely on your device,
   against reference data built into the download. There is no server
   doing the science, no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no
   advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party
   SDK of any kind. The app contains no logging code at all.

   The app has exactly one network feature, and it is optional and off
   until you switch it on: syncing your own records between your own
   devices through your own private iCloud database (Section 4).

   We have declared "Data Not Collected" on the App Store, and the
   privacy manifest shipped inside the app says the same thing.


2. WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

   Spectromaster saves your work inside the app's own private storage
   on your device:

   - Solutions (samples) and their labels, comments and notes.
   - Measurements: peak tables, multiplets, and the interpretations
     produced from them.
   - Spectrum photographs you captured or imported, and instrument
     files you opened.
   - A history of readings, and records of what you have deleted, so a
     deletion is not undone the next time your devices sync.
   - App preferences, and a cached flag recording whether you own the
     unlock.

   On iPhone and iPad this data is written with file protection
   enabled, so it stays encrypted while the device is locked.

   You can delete any of it from within the app, or remove all of it by
   deleting the app. Deleting the app does not cancel a purchase (see
   Section 6).


3. NETWORK USE

   No stage of the science touches the network. Extracting peaks,
   measuring, interpreting, ranking candidates and searching the
   reference library all run locally against data compiled into the
   app. A result is reproducible on a device in aeroplane mode, and the
   app contains no general-purpose networking code.

   We do not fetch reference data at runtime, and nothing about your
   use of the app is reported to us.

   The only network activity is:

   - iCloud sync, if you switch it on (Section 4).
   - The purchase and purchase-restore flow, performed by Apple's own
     store services rather than by us (Section 6).


4. ICLOUD SYNC (OPTIONAL)

   Sync is off until you turn it on, and the app is fully usable with
   it left off forever. Nothing in the science or the interpretation
   depends on it.

   When it is on, your records — solutions, measurements, spectrum
   photographs, history, and deletion records — are copied between your
   own devices through the PRIVATE database of your own iCloud account.

   What this means in practice:

   - The data goes to your Apple account, not to us. We have no server
     and no database, and we have no way to read, request, or recover
     any of it.
   - Only the private database is used. The container's public database
     is never touched, so nothing you record is visible to other users
     or to us.
   - It syncs between your devices only. There is no sharing feature,
     no collaboration, and no other person's device involved.
   - Turning sync off stops further copying. Records already in your
     iCloud remain there under your control, and can be removed by
     deleting the corresponding solutions in the app, or through your
     Apple account's iCloud storage settings.

   Deleting a solution, a measurement, or a photograph in the app
   deletes the matching record and image from your iCloud on the next
   sync, and records the deletion so that another device does not put
   it back.

   Apple processes iCloud data under its own privacy policy, which we
   do not control: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/


5. CAMERA AND PHOTOGRAPHS

   Spectromaster can photograph a printed or on-screen spectrum so it
   can be read. The camera is used only when you open it, and images go
   straight into the app's own storage.

   - Location is never recorded. The app does not use location services
     at all, and every photograph it stores is re-encoded with its
     metadata removed: no GPS coordinates, no device or lens
     information, no capture timestamps from the camera.
   - Access to your photo library is ADD-ONLY. The app can save a
     capture to your library when you ask it to, and it has no
     permission to read your library or to see any other photograph in
     it.
   - Importing a spectrum image or an instrument file reads only the
     file you chose.

   On the Mac, the app has no camera or photo-library access at all.


6. PURCHASES

   Spectromaster offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase that
   removes the limit on how many solutions you can keep at once and
   enables the iCloud sync described in Section 4.

   Reading spectra, every analysis and interpretation, and exporting
   your own records are free and unlimited, permanently. Nothing about
   the accuracy of a result depends on whether you have paid.

   Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store
   your payment details, your Apple Account identity, or your purchase
   history. Whether you currently own the unlock is checked on device
   through Apple's own store APIs; there is no receipt server, and the
   check involves no service of ours.

   Apple handles that transaction under its own privacy policy, which
   we do not control: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

   To restore a previous purchase on a new device or after
   reinstalling, use Restore Purchase.


7. EXPORTS AND BACKUPS

   You can export your records and back up the whole cabinet at any
   time, in the free version as well as the paid one, including after a
   refund. Your measurements are yours and are never held hostage.

   Exports are written where you choose, by you. They contain your
   records and nothing else: no device identifiers, no account
   information, and no usage data. They are not sent anywhere.


8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

   Spectromaster is a laboratory and study tool for chemistry, not a
   product directed at children. Because it collects no personal data
   at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children, and
   there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request the deletion
   of. Data the app has written is on the device, under your control,
   and is removed with the app.


9. YOUR RIGHTS

   Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights of
   access, correction, deletion, and portability over personal data a
   company holds about you.

   We hold none. There is no account, no server, and no database in
   which you appear, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct,
   export, or erase. The data described in Section 2 is under your own
   control on your own device, and the iCloud copy described in Section
   4 is in your own Apple account.

   We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not
   collect any.


10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

   If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this
   URL with a new version date. Material changes will also be noted in
   the app's release notes.

   Any widening of the single network exception in Section 4 would be a
   material change and would be stated here plainly, not slipped in.
